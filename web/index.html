Privacy and setup notes

  • Camera frames stay in the browser; no server upload is required.
  • Run from localhost or HTTPS so the browser allows camera access.
  • Click anywhere on the page to initialize camera detection.
  • Show one hand or both hands to draw separate detection boxes.
  • Press Esc to stop the camera.
